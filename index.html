Before connecting

Three quick steps

  1. 1

    Sign in, then select Prepare secure installer.

  2. 2

    Select Connect and install, choose the correct COM port, then choose Erase.

  3. 3

    When “Preparing installation” appears, hold BOOT and tap RESET/EN. Release BOOT when “Erasing” begins, then keep USB connected until installation completes.

Firmware is loaded from the configured Firebase release. This page does not enable Secure Boot, Flash Encryption, or modify security eFuses.
